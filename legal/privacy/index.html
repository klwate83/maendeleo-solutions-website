Website Privacy Policy

Draft dated 5 November 2026 — not effective until approved and published.

This draft notice applies to Maendeleo Solutions' informational portfolio website, which showcases skills, projects and services. It is not designed as a user-account platform or customer-data repository. Individual applications have separate privacy notices.

Who is responsible

Maendeleo Solutions is responsible for personal information it processes through its website and direct business enquiries. Contact us using the details below for privacy requests.

Information we may receive

The website does not offer visitor accounts or a customer-profile database. If you voluntarily contact Maendeleo Solutions by email or through the website's EmailJS-powered enquiry feature, your message and the contact details you submit are transmitted through the email delivery service to enable us to receive and respond to your enquiry. Maendeleo Solutions does not use the website as a visitor-account or customer-profile database. Hosting and other technical providers may process routine service information.

Purposes

Any enquiry information is used to respond to the enquiry, discuss potential work, maintain service security and meet applicable legal obligations. The website is primarily a portfolio and informational resource.

Service providers and international transfers

The website uses EmailJS for enquiry delivery. EmailJS, the destination email provider and website hosting providers may process information required to deliver messages or operate the website, potentially in other countries. The exact EmailJS configuration, provider terms, hosting logs and cross-border transfer arrangements must be checked before publication.

Retention

The website is not intended to retain a visitor-profile database. Enquiry emails and related correspondence may nevertheless remain in email systems for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records and meet legal obligations. EmailJS/provider retention settings and any mailbox retention practices must be verified before publication.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing or raise a complaint with the relevant regulator. Contact us using the address below.

Cookies and analytics

The site's EmailJS integration and hosting may use technical storage or request logs. We have not verified whether additional cookies, analytics or embedded services are active; this must be checked against the deployed website before publication.

Updates

We will update this notice when our website or data-processing practices change.
