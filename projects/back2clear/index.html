Product Overview

Private progress, made visible

Back2Clear is a native Android wellness application designed to support adults who want to stop vaping. Users can choose an exact quit date and time, monitor their vape-free duration, calculate money saved, set a personal reward goal, review carefully worded nicotine-withdrawal milestones and receive optional daily encouragement.

The experience avoids shame-based language and does not present smoking-specific recovery timelines as proven vaping outcomes. User profile and progress data remain locally stored on the device.

Designed and developed by Maendeleo Solutions.

User Problem

Progress can feel invisible

Stopping vaping is a personal decision, yet the early benefits can be difficult to see. Generic trackers may rely on accounts, use shame-based language, or repeat smoking-specific health claims as though they were proven vaping outcomes.

Product Approach

Calm, measurable support

Back2Clear keeps the experience private and practical: clear timers, savings projections, personal rewards, optional encouragement and carefully framed wellness information—all without a login or cloud profile.

Privacy Approach

Local by design

Profile and progress information stays on the Android device. Back2Clear has no login or cloud profile, allows local data deletion and keeps advertising consent separate from quitting data.

Read the Back2Clear Privacy Policy →

Health-Content Approach

Evidence-conscious information

Back2Clear provides general wellness and nicotine-cessation information without presenting smoking-specific recovery timelines as established vaping outcomes. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.
