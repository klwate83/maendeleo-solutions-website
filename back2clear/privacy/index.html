Back2Clear Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Back2Clear is a privacy-first Android application that helps people track progress toward stopping vaping. This policy explains what the app stores, how advertising services may process information and the choices available to users.

Information stored on the device

Back2Clear stores the following information locally on the user’s Android device:

  • Age
  • Lifestyle selections
  • Vaping spend
  • Currency
  • Quit dates and times
  • Previous starts
  • Reminder settings
  • Selected colour theme
  • Reward name and target
  • Calculated duration, savings and milestone progress

Maendeleo Solutions does not currently receive this quitting profile. Back2Clear does not require an account or create a cloud profile. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app’s profile data.

Advertising and consent

The free version of Back2Clear may use Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform.

Back2Clear does not supply the following information to the advertising SDK for ad targeting:

  • Age entered in the app
  • Lifestyle selections
  • Vaping spend
  • Currency
  • Quit dates or times
  • Previous starts
  • Savings or milestone progress
  • Reward goals
  • Notification history

Depending on the user’s region, consent choices, application configuration and Google services, Google may process limited information such as device identifiers, diagnostics, approximate location derived from an IP address and advertising interactions. This information may be used for advertisement delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where required, Back2Clear presents consent choices before requesting an advertisement. Eligible users can revisit those choices using “Ad privacy choices” in the app.

Advertising is restricted to subtle inline banners at the end of the Home and Journey screens. Advertisements are not permitted during onboarding, quit-date selection, Settings, data deletion or encouragement notifications.

Notifications

If enabled, Back2Clear schedules local daily or weekly encouragement notifications using Android background services.

Notifications never contain advertisements.

Users can change the reminder frequency or disable notifications in Back2Clear Settings and in Android system settings.

Health and wellness information

Back2Clear provides general wellness and nicotine-cessation information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

Vaping-specific recovery timelines are still being researched. Back2Clear therefore does not present smoking-only carbon-monoxide, cardiovascular, lung-function or organ-recovery timelines as established vaping outcomes.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional about nicotine cessation, withdrawal symptoms or medical concerns.

Data retention and deletion

The locally stored quitting profile remains on the user’s device until the user deletes it or removes the application.

Users can select “Delete all local data” in Back2Clear Settings. This removes the locally stored profile, quit history, spending information, rewards and progress from that device.

Advertising consent records managed by Google may require separate management through “Ad privacy choices,” Android settings or Google services.

Children and intended audience

Back2Clear is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to children.

Security

Back2Clear limits the quitting profile to local device storage and disables Android cloud backup for that data. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, but the application is designed to minimise collection and external transmission.

Changes to this policy

Maendeleo Solutions may update this policy when Back2Clear’s features, integrations or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.
